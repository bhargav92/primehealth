Welcome to PrimeHealth.in, India's largest digital healthcare platform.

Our mission is to provide customers with 24/7 access to high-quality healthcare services. We connect patients with top-tier diagnostic centers for medical tests, hospitals for doctor consultations and surgeries, and wellness facilities across India, Singapore, Indonesia, Thailand, Italy, and Dubai, ensuring that you receive the best possible care at affordable prices.

At PrimeHealthin, we understand the importance of comprehensive healthcare services. That’s why we offer:

Doctor Consultations: Access to experienced doctors across various specialties for both in-person and Video consultations.

Diagnostics: A wide range of tests including laboratory tests, imaging, and specialized and advanced genetic screenings to help diagnose and monitor health conditions.

Surgeries: Arrangements for elective and emergency surgeries with top hospitals and surgeons, ensuring quality care and patient safety. We offer cost-effective options, multiple estimations, and second opinions with top doctors. We help patients minimize out-of-pocket expenses by partnering with major hospital chains that offer cashless options and extensive insurance networks.

Corporate Health Checks: Customizable health check packages tailored for corporate employees to promote workplace wellness. Corporations can maximize the benefits from our extensive network of service providers.

Medical and Wellness Tourism: Facilitating medical and wellness tourism by connecting international patients with advanced medical infrastructure and skilled professionals across India, Singapore, Thailand, Italy, and Dubai.

Our vision is to become the leading healthcare service aggregator, renowned for our commitment to excellence, innovation, and patient-centric care.
